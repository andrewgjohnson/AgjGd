# [AgjGd](/methods/)::[imageblendedcolorallocate](/methods/imageblendedcolorallocate/) Example: Opacity

The `$opacityColor1` parameter controls how much of each color ends up in the blend. The same blue and cyan are blended three times — at 75%, 50% and 25% blue — and the five colors are drawn together as a progression running from cyan through to blue.
